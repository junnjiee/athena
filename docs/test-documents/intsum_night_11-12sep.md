# INTSUM 11/12 SEP — Night Period

**Originator:** Brigade Intelligence Cell
**Sources:** UAV feed (Sector North), SIGINT intercept 112340H
**Handling:** Exercise material. All units, places and events are fictional.

## 1. Reserve Dispositions

### 1.1 Tengah

UAV imagery at 112210H positively identified **3rd Mechanised Battalion**
in a dispersed laager at **Tengah**. Vehicle count: 24 x BTR-90, 10 x T-72,
6 x soft-skin trucks. This independently corroborates the ground observation
reported by 2 SIB. The battalion is held at K3 (battalion reserve) under
21st Motor Rifle Brigade.

### 1.2 Sarimbun

SIGINT intercept at 112340H places the **Divisional Reserve Tank Regiment**
in the vicinity of **Sarimbun**. Call-sign analysis indicates a regimental
headquarters plus at least two tank battalions. This is the division-level
reserve (K4). Owning formation: 4th Guards Tank Division. No notice-to-move
timings available.

## 2. Indicators and Warnings

- Increased radio traffic between Sarimbun and the divisional HQ net.
- Bridging equipment observed moving toward the western sector.

## 3. Assessment

The divisional reserve at Sarimbun is the decisive element. If committed, it
would move south along the coastal axis toward Lim Chu Kang.
