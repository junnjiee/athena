CLASSIFICATION: EXERCISE - UNCLASSIFIED (FICTIONAL TRAINING MATERIAL)

1. ENEMY SITUATION

a. 3rd Mechanised Battalion (3 MECH BN), subordinate to 21st Motor Rifle Brigade,
   is located in a laager position in the vicinity of TENGAH. Assessed as the
   battalion reserve (K3) for the brigade sector. Observed composition at last
   light: two mechanised infantry companies on BTR-90, one tank company with
   10 x T-72, and a mortar platoon. Battalion has been static for 18 hours.

b. Recce Company 2/14 is operating from a hide in the vicinity of MURAI.
   Assessed as local reinforcement (K1). Strength assessed as one platoon of
   BRDM-2 scout cars (4 vehicles) plus a dismounted section.

c. Enemy notice-to-move for the battalion reserve is assessed at 30 minutes
   decision time and 45 minutes readiness, based on the observed dispersion
   of the laager.

2. ASSESSMENT
   3 MECH BN remains the most capable reserve in the sector and is positioned
   to reinforce north along Lim Chu Kang Road within one hour of commencement.

END SITREP
